Therefore, the stage was shortened from 133 to 88.7 kilometers. AFter the team presentation in Thoiry, the riders were transfered to Paris by bus. There, the peloton did 6 laps on the Champs-Élysées, followed by the already planned 3 laps over the Montmartre climb. GC times were taken at the 5th passage of the finish line.
